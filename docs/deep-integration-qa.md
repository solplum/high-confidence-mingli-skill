## Exact limits

BaZi ST01–ST05 are narrow, explicit project conjunctions, not a universal strong/weak classifier or a fully established classical algorithm. They compare seasonal context, operational same-element roots and support/opposition; disputed efficacy and unassessed 刑害 remain visible. Complete 制化, 司令, 土月/土日主, special patterns, 调候 and unique 喜用神 are not implemented. A conditional tendency does not authorize personal-event conclusions.

Ziwei uses 14 bounded modern-source star themes and 2 concepts, actual four-palace positions, brightness and counterchecks. Its two thematic combinations are project syntheses, not classical patterns. Complete transformations, borrowed-star interpretation, supporting-star combinations and timing are missing. Neither module constitutes a complete life reading or validated personality measurement.

Scenario coverage remains model-specific: civil/mean-solar boundaries are complete only within the enumerated model, while apparent-solar outputs are numerical witnesses and not exhaustive. Full Ziwei/Qizheng recalculation, qiyun/dayun ages, alternative conventions and astronomical-model error are excluded. No TypeScript static typecheck was run; Node validation used tsx. Sources and deterministic tests establish traceability/implementation consistency, not scientific or predictive validity.
